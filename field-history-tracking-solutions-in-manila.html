What is Salesforce Field History Tracking?

Salesforce Field History Tracking (FHT) is a Salesforce feature that records changes to selected standard and custom fields while also capturing the previous and new updated values along with user and time details. This feature helps organizations track data updates accurately. This feature is also essential for compliance, reporting and operational transparency. Manila businesses benefit from expert guidance to configure it correctly.

  • Change Recording :- FHT logs every update that is made to the tracked fields, capturing old and new values along with precise timestamps.
  • User Identification :- It clearly identifies the user who is responsible for each data change, ensuring accountability and transparency.
  • Audit-Friendly Logs :- FHT helps maintain reliable and structured data history to support audits, compliance and governance needs.
  • Error Tracking :-It helps teams quickly identify incorrect, accidental or unauthorized data changes for timely correction.
  • Reporting Access :-FHT enables easy access to tracked field history data through standard Salesforce reports and analytics.
  • Local Implementation :- It is designed for businesses that handle frequent or high-value transactions that require strict oversight.
