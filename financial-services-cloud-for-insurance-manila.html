  • Household and relationship mapping:- FSC heps you track family members, beneficiaries, dependents and business relationships clearly.

Insurance Workflows You Can Manage with Salesforce FSC

Insurance companies handle multiple workflows simultaneously, like sales, renewals, claims, service requests, compliance and much more. Salesforce FSC helps to make it easier to manage all these workflows with clear tracking and automation processes. It helps to improve coordination between sales teams, brokers and support departments for a seamless experience. Common FSC use cases in insurance:

  • Policy sales and lead tracking:- You can track every lead from the first inquiry to policy issuance, giving agents a 360-degree view of customer details and next steps.
