What is Salesforce Field History Tracking?

Salesforce Field History Tracking (FHT) is a feature that monitors and records changes made to the selected fields within standard and custom objects. Whenever a tracked field is updated, Salesforce captures everything, whether it is the old value, new value, who and when they made the update. This provides complete data evolution visibility to the organizations, which is important for data governance, auditing and trust.

  • Automatic tracking of key CRM fields :- Salesforce automatically logs every tracked field change in real time while ensuring no critical update goes unnoticed.
  • Clear and complete audit trail :- FHT documents every modification and making it easy to track entire data update history during internal reviews and external audits.
  • Supports standard and custom objects :- With FHT you can track everything whether it is leads, accounts, opportunities, cases and more.
  • Captures both manual and automated updates :-You Every change made by users, workflows, flows, approvals or integrations is captured to ensure nothing goes unnoticed by the system.
  • Easy setup and no coding needed :-Administrators can enable FHT directly from object settings, making it accessible even without technical expertise.
  • Perfect for data-sensitive and high-volume industries :- It is designed for businesses that handle frequent or high-value transactions that require strict oversight.

What Fields Can Be Tracked?

Salesforce enables organizations to track up to 20 fields in one object and authorizes businesses to monitor the fields that matter most. Companies in Philippines can benefit from tracking everything from customer details to financial values and operational stages. Examples of some trackable fields:

  • Customer & Contact Details :- With FHT you can track updates to phones, emails, names and addresses to keep the customer profiles accurate.
  • Sales Pipeline & Values :- You can monitor stage changes, deal amounts, close dates and probabilities to ensure accurate sales forecasts.
  • Customer support case updates :-You can monitor changes in status, priority, ownership and resolution details and ensure accountability within support teams.
  • Financial and Compliance Data :-You can monitor sensitive fields like loan approvals, policy updates, monetary values and credit limits to support compliance requirements.
  • Real Estate Records :- With FHT track the changes to property status, pricing updates, booking stages and document submissions to ensure transparent customer communication.
  • Student & Academic Information :- Educational institutions can track enrollment stages, application progress, academic changes and administrative updates for better record accuracy.
